# Notes from Armando

This folder captures industry knowledge from Armando's 25+ years in medical devices and pharma. The goal: turn ephemeral hallway-conversation insights into durable written knowledge that informs the empirical strategy and grounds the paper's institutional details.

## What goes here

Anything Armando knows that Robert (or any outside reader of the paper) would not learn from a textbook. Examples:

- How an FDA inspection actually unfolds, from a manufacturer's POV
- Why some firms get 483s but never a warning letter
- The internal IR / legal calculus around how to disclose a warning letter
- Differences in how Class I vs. Class II vs. Class III device makers experience FDA scrutiny
- War stories: specific (anonymized) instances of FDA letters and how firms responded
- Vocabulary translation: "What does CGMP really mean on the factory floor?"

## How to capture

- **One topic per file.** Naming: `YYYY-MM-DD_short-topic.md` (e.g., `2026-05-21_warning-letter-disclosure-decision.md`).
- **Front-load the takeaway.** Open with a one-line statement of the insight. Then elaborate.
- **Mark hypotheses.** If a note suggests an empirical prediction, flag it explicitly: `**Empirical prediction:** ...`
- **Mark questions.** If a note opens questions, flag them: `**Open question:** ...`

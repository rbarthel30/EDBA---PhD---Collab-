## I. SEC Comment Letters — the Methodological Template

The FDA-comment-letter setting is the medical-device analog to the SEC-comment-letter literature. These are the papers to study most carefully for design choices.

- **Cassell, C. A., Dreher, L. M., & Myers, L. A. (2013).** "Reviewing the SEC's Review Process: 10-K Comment Letters and the Cost of Remediation." *The Accounting Review* 88(6), 1875–1908.
- **Bozanic, Z., Dietrich, J. R., & Johnson, B. A. (2017).** "SEC Comment Letters and Firm Disclosure." *Journal of Accounting and Public Policy* 36(5), 337–357.
- **Johnston, R., & Petacchi, R. (2017).** "Regulatory Oversight of Financial Reporting: Securities and Exchange Commission Comment Letters." *Contemporary Accounting Research* 34(2), 1128–1155.
- **Dechow, P. M., Lawrence, A., & Ryans, J. P. (2016).** "SEC Comment Letters and Insider Sales." *The Accounting Review* 91(2), 401–439.
- **Brown, S. V., Tian, X. S., & Wu Tucker, J. (2018).** "The Spillover Effect of SEC Comment Letters on Qualitative Corporate Disclosure." *Contemporary Accounting Research* 35(2), 622–656.
